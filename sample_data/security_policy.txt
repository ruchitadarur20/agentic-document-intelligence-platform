Acme Financial Services Information Security Policy

All customer financial documents must be classified before storage. Documents containing
account numbers, social security numbers, loan applications, tax forms, or personal
financial statements are classified as confidential.

Confidential documents must be encrypted at rest and in transit. Access is limited to
authorized employees with a documented business purpose. Every document access event
must be logged with the user identifier, timestamp, document identifier, and action.

The retention period for loan application documents is seven years after account closure.
Documents under litigation hold must not be deleted until the legal department removes
the hold. Quarterly audits must verify retention controls and access control evidence.

AI-generated summaries must cite the source document chunks used to produce each answer.
Unsupported claims must be flagged for human review before the answer is sent to a
customer or regulator.
