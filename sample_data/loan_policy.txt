Acme Lending Operations Policy

Loan applications must include identity verification, income documentation, and a signed
borrower consent form before underwriting can begin. Missing income documentation must
be routed to manual review within one business day.

Approved loan packages must be retained for seven years after account closure. Rejected
loan applications must be retained for three years after the final decision date.

AI-generated borrower summaries must cite the exact source document chunks used for
the answer. If the source evidence is incomplete, the summary must clearly state that
manual review is required before the response is sent to the borrower.
