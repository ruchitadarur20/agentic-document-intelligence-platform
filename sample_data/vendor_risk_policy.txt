Acme Third-Party Vendor Risk Standard

New vendors that process confidential customer information require security review
before contract approval. The review must include SOC 2 evidence, data retention terms,
incident notification commitments, and encryption controls.

Critical vendors must be reassessed every twelve months. High-risk findings require a
remediation owner, target date, and executive risk acceptance if the finding remains open
for more than thirty days.

Vendor risk summaries generated by AI must include citations to the reviewed evidence.
Unsupported claims about certifications, breach history, or control effectiveness must be
flagged for human validation.
