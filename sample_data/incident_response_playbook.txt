Acme Incident Response Playbook

Suspected exposure of confidential customer data must be escalated to the security
incident commander within fifteen minutes. The initial triage record must include the
affected system, discovered time, suspected data type, and current containment status.

Customer-impacting incidents require legal and compliance review before any external
notification is sent. Evidence logs, timeline notes, and containment actions must be
preserved for audit review for six years.

AI-assisted incident briefs must cite the evidence used for every timeline and impact
statement. Unverified root-cause statements must be labeled as preliminary.
