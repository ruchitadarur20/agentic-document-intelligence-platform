Acme Employee Onboarding Policy

New employees must complete identity verification, security awareness training, and
acceptable-use acknowledgement before receiving production access. Managers must confirm
role-based access requirements before the access request is approved.

Contractor onboarding requires a sponsor, end date, and quarterly access review. Access
for contractors must expire automatically on the contract end date unless renewed by the
sponsor.

AI-generated onboarding answers must cite policy evidence and must not infer employment
eligibility, background-check results, or access approval without supporting records.
